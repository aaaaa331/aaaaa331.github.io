Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Minori Content Tools (the App) is a personal-use tool that helps its own operator upload video content to their own TikTok account as drafts.

What we collect

The App only accesses the TikTok account that explicitly authorizes it via TikTok OAuth. We request the minimum scope needed to upload video content as a draft. We do not collect, store, or share data about any other TikTok users.

How we use it

Access tokens are used solely to upload video files, captions, and cover images created by the operator, to the authorizing account's TikTok draft inbox. Tokens are stored securely and only on the operator's own device.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner via TikTok.
